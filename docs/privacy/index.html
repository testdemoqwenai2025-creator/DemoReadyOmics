Privacy & Compliance

Tessera Bio takes privacy and regulatory compliance seriously. This page documents what we collect, how we handle it, and which regulations we comply with. It is intentionally short — privacy pages that aren't read don't protect anyone.

What we collect

Your email if you sign up for the private beta. That's it.

We do not track analytics on this site. No Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Vercel analytics. We do not use cookies except for the theme toggle (which uses localStorage, not a cookie). We do not run advertising pixels. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

When you email us, we store your email and your message in our email provider (currently Gmail) for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our conversation. You can request deletion of any stored email at any time.

GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)

Tessera Bio is the data controller for the personal data we process (your email address). Our lawful basis for processing is legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) — you contacted us, and we have a legitimate interest in responding and maintaining a record of our conversation.

  • Right of access (Art. 15): you can request a copy of any data we hold about you by emailing privacy@tessera.bio.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): you can request deletion of your email and any associated messages at any time.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): you can object to processing for direct marketing at any time.
  • Response time: we will respond to any data subject request within 30 days, as required by Art. 12(3).
  • Data transfer outside the EEA: we do not transfer personal data outside the EEA. If we ever need to, we will update this page and obtain explicit consent first.

HIPAA (US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)

Tessera does not store Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under HIPAA §160.103.

  • Public datasets we ingest (GEO, ArrayExpress, DepMap, Ensembl, UniProt, etc.) are not PHI under HIPAA. They are research datasets released under open licences.
  • Controlled-access datasets (GTEx, TCGA controlled tier, dbGaP-controlled studies) require the user to bring their own dbGaP authorisation. Tessera never sees the underlying data; we only see the metadata that is publicly available on the dbGaP / GTEx portals. The user remains the HIPAA-covered entity for their use of the controlled data.
  • If Tessera ever proposes to handle PHI directly (e.g., for a clinical-tier product), we will sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all relevant sub-processors and publish a HIPAA readiness statement here before any such product launch.

CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

  • We do not sell personal information (CCPA §1798.140(ad)).
  • We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to delete (§1798.105): California residents can request deletion by emailing privacy@tessera.bio.
  • Right to know (§1798.100): California residents can request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect.

21 CFR Part 11 (FDA Electronic Records)

Not yet applicable. Tessera is in private beta for research use, not clinical use. 21 CFR Part 11 applies to electronic records used in FDA-regulated activities; we are not yet in that scope.

However, the Tessera pipeline already implements several Part 11 principles by design:

  • Audit trail (§11.10(e)): every dataset card records the pipeline commit, container hash, and tool versions used to produce it.
  • Record retention (§11.10(c)): dataset cards are content-addressed (SHA-256) and remain permanently accessible at their version-pinned URL.
  • Document controls (§11.50): every release is signed (currently SHA-256 stub; production will use Sigstore / cosign).

We will publish a full Part 11 readiness statement before any clinical-tier product launch.
