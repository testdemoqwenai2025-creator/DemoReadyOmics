About Tessera Bio / ReadyOmics

The wedge, the story, and where we're going.

Tessera Bio builds the layer that turns public biological data into ML-ready, harmonised, version-controlled training datasets. This page is for investors, partners, and prospects who want the longer version of what we do and why.

The story

The Tessera Bio thesis started with a simple observation: ML teams in drug discovery spend 60–80% of their time producing clean, harmonised training datasets, and almost no platform ships datasets that are ready to train on. Existing bioinformatics platforms (Nextflow, Terra, Galaxy, Seqera, AWS HealthOmics) are good at running workflows. Almost none of them ship the layer above the workflows — the dataset card, the leakage-controlled splits, the identifier-drift report, the signed provenance.

That layer requires biology depth, not just workflow engineering. It's where a small team with the right expertise can build something incumbents structurally cannot easily replicate. That's the wedge.

The name comes from tessera — a small piece of a mosaic. Public biological data is a mosaic of identifiers, releases, and licences. Tessera Bio is the layer that joins the pieces into a coherent picture that an ML team can train on.

The wedge

Three differentiators, in priority order:

U1
ML-ready outputs

Every pipeline emits a model-ready matrix plus a dataset card. Train/val/test splits are stratified by biological leakage — held-out donors, families, chromosomes — not random.

U2
Identifier stability

Every dataset is pinned to a specific Ensembl / UniProt release. A built-in identifier-drift report flags what breaks when those releases change, and which models need retraining.

U3
Multi-omic harmonisation

Genes, transcripts, proteins, metabolites, pathways are first-class, joined, versioned citizens. v0.1 is RNA-seq only; multi-omic ships in v0.2.

For more detail, read the full differentiators section or the documentation hub.
