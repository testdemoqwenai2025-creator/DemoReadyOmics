Known biases & limitations

  • Cell line expression differs from primary tumour expression; models trained here may not transfer.
  • Test lineages are underrepresented in training; performance on rare lineages will be lower.

Suggested & discouraged uses

Suggested

  • Drug response prediction benchmarking (cell-line-only).
  • Cross-lineage transfer learning experiments.

Discouraged

  • Do not use for patient-level predictions without external validation.
